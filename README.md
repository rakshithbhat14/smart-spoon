# smart-spoon
The Smart Spoon integrates AI and machine learning algorithms to deliver a personalized tasting experience tailored to the user’s health profile, food type, and feedback history. The system utilizes a compact electrical stimulation unit that interacts with taste receptors via microcurrents, altering ion channel activity in the tongue to trigger flavor perception. Through a food recognition module, powered by computer vision, the device can analyze the uploaded image of the user’s meal to estimate its salt content and automatically calibrate stimulation intensity. The accompanying Streamlit-based application allows users to explore three core features: food recognition and salt estimation, market research insights from a curated dataset of 200+ survey responses, and sentiment analysis of user feedback using natural language processing. The system’s ability to adapt stimulation levels based on dietary needs, age group, and individual sensory response makes it uniquely suitable for diverse populations including the elderly, fitness enthusiasts, and patients with chronic health conditions.
